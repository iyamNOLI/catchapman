Haddrell’s Honey Snap Pack for Kids

As pioneers of New Zealand’s precious mānuka honey, Haddrell’s believes in the unique properties of this natural product. Haddrell’s specialises only in UMF™ mānuka honey for guaranteed authenticity, and care about sustaining the future of this precious honey.

Working with direction from Curious Design we created an energetic, bright bee mascot to appeal to children and emphasis how delicious the honey is in these easy to eat, on-the-go snack packs.
