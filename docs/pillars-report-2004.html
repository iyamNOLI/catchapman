Pillars Ka Pou Whakahou

Pillars Ka Pou Whakahou is a charity that exists to support the tamariki and whānau of people in prison to create positive futures for themselves. Pillars mahi provides programmes and wrap-around social work services for whānau and mentoring for children.

Working alongside design studio Stir Creative, we created a 28 page annual report highlighting the immense work and achievement of both Pillars staff, volunteers, whānau, and their donors. The illustrations are uplifting and positive, using bold, bright colours and engaging characters to convey the key messages of this annual report.
