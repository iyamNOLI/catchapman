Ecostore

Established in 1993, Ecostore develops, manufactures and packs all their household and personal care products at their own carboNZero certified factory in Auckland and share them worldwide.

Working alongside Ecostore's marketing team and an animation company, my illustrations have been turned into an ongoing series of animated web stories. Each features the 'Little Bits Of Good'  Ecostore are doing as a company both locally and globally. This includes their contribution to Habitat for Humanity, a commitment to recycled, reusable, reduced packaging and a focus on being a carboNZero company, amongst many other great initiatives.
