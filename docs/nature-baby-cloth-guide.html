Nature Baby

Nature Baby specialises in innovative, organic and chemical free products that are good for you, your baby and nature.

The focus of this project was to create a fold out poster to illustrate the steps to using a cloth nappy, that the chief nappy changers could keep and refer to. The illustrations deliver the message in a sweet, attractive, yet informative way.
