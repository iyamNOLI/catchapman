Craggy Range Sheep Dairy

Award-winning sheep dairy producers Craggy Range Sheep Dairy, create artisan food products in New Zealand. Ethically-farmed, Craggy Range Sheep Dairy lambs stay with their mums, lounging under shady trees on the grassy hills of Craggy Range, until naturally weaned. A2 sheep milk can be gentler on the stomach, higher in amino acids, protein, iron – and has almost twice the calcium as cow milk. 

Working alongside Snapfire Design, we chose a classic etching style to draw Martha, one of the farms matriarch dairy sheep, becoming the identity of Craggy Range’s logo and branding.
