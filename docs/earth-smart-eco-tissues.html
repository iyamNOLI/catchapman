Earth Smart

New Zealand company EarthSmart removed the plastic from the packaging of their 100% recyclable facial tissues. Made from Rapidly Renewable Fibre (RRF) EarthSmart plant fast growing trees and harvest them every 6 years to save our planets natural resources.

Directed by Curious Design, I worked to create a collection of attractive flora and fauna in a watercolour and gauche style, helping create attractive outer packaging for everyday products that customers would be happy to have on display in their homes.
