Ecostore

Established in 1993, Ecostore develops, manufactures and packs all their household and personal care products at their own carboNZero certified factory in Auckland and share them worldwide.

Following the effort from all New Zealanders to keep each other safe through the Covid-19 Pandemic, Ecostore launched an online campaign to show what they are doing to help make a better future. Working with their digital marketing team we created a series of hand drawn texts and illustrations that have been animated to tell the story of Ecostore being Here For You, their Team and the Community.
