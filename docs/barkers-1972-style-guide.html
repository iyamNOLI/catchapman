Barkers 1972 Magazine

The Barkers 1972 Magazine and Journal are curated to showcase the latest Barkers menswear ranges, keep clients up to date on the latest trends, along with providing interesting and varied insights into local people and projects of interest.

"As a distinctly local business, we’re constantly inspired by the unique talent that surrounds us in this small country. Skill is something we foster and celebrate, and that dialogue of pride, success and hard work sees us joining forces with everyone from the All Blacks and Swanndri to Surfline and Volvo."

These publications have a high production value and frequently commission illustrations to help tie together the features.
