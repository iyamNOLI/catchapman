Birdy

Working with creative design studio Whitelaw Mitchell, I was commissioned to develop a series of branding illustrations for Birdy, a champagne & cocktail bar in Queenstown. Birdy has an intimate art deco speakeasy aesthetic and the illustration concept to accompany this were for traditional cherubs, but with mischievous personalities shining through. Each cherub represented a different facet of the Birdy experience, capturing its cheeky nature and essence.
