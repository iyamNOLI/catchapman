Nature Baby

Nature Baby specialises in innovative, organic and chemical free products that are good for you, your baby and nature.

The focus of this project was to create a pamphlet to illustrate the steps to using the Nature Baby Ring Sling. The illustrations accompany simple text to deliver the instructions in a clear and attractive way, sympathetic to Nature Baby's aesthetic.
