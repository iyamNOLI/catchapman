Metro Magazine

Metro is the go-to magazine, in print and online, for Aucklanders who want to connect with their city on a deeper level. Covering where to eat out, the arts, design, current affairs, education and social trends, with content that’s insightful, edgy, informed, entertaining and stylish.

The illustrations to accompany the article "The Trouble With Auckland Transport", were asked to be “Scarry-esque” and emphasis the current chaotic interaction of busy traffic and roads, pedestrians and businesses in the inner city, in a funny light hearted way.
