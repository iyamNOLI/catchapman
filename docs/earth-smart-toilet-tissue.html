Earth Smart

Made in New Zealand, EarthSmart's Toilet Tissue packaging contains no plastic coating or layers and is kerbside recyclable. The 100% recycled paper tissue is not bleached with chlorine during recycling, has no added dyes & fragrances, is biodegradable and safe in septic tanks.

Directed by Curious Design, I worked to create a collection of attractive flora and fauna in a watercolour and gauche style, helping create attractive outer packaging for everyday products that customers would be happy to have on display in their homes.
