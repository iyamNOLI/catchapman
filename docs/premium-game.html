Premium Game

Specialising in South Island wild game, Premium Game is MPI certified wild meat harvested and processed in the most natural way. Audited by AssureQuality, each animal is traced to the land they have come and have never been subject to Glyphosate, GMO, Hormones or Antibiotics.

Premium Game required a series of illustrations of wild game, at the time aesthetically in keeping with their existing logo. Following the success of the illustrations I drew, I went on to update their company logo.  The illustrations needed to be bold enough to be used in both small print and larger promotional material, yet with enough detail to illustrate the lean muscular quality of these animals.
